# GENOMORPH — GAME FLOW (SIMPLE VERSION)

Short sentences. Step by step. Easy to scan.

---

## THE SETUP

You are "the Volunteer."

You test a special suit called the **GenoMorph Suit**.

It holds 12 animal genomes.

It lets you transform into any of them.

During your first test, the suit breaks.

All 12 genomes leak out into the wetland.

Nearby animals absorb them.

Your suit is now stuck. Frog only.

Dr. Sterling talks to you by radio.

He guides you. He does not solve things for you.

---

## THE CORE LOOP

This loop repeats through the whole game:

1. Explore as your current animal
2. Find something damaged
3. Investigate it
4. Learn which animal can fix it
5. Fight or solve the problem
6. Find that animal's genome
7. Transform
8. Repeat

Once you get this loop, you understand the whole game.

## ACT 1 — WETLANDS

This is the only fully-designed part right now.

### Step 1 — Wake Up

You wake up as Frog.

Sterling checks in briefly.

You practice moving. No danger yet.

### Step 2 — Something Is Wrong

You look around.

You see:
- Dirty water
- Dead plants
- Human trash

You feel it. This place is broken.

### Step 3 — You Choose What to Check First

Three things you can investigate:
- The dirty water
- The dead plants
- The human trash

You pick one. Any order.

Each one gives you a different short reaction.

All three lead to the same next step.

### Step 4 — First Fight

Sterling reacts to what you found.

One **Mosquito** shows up.

You fight it.

This teaches you:
- Basic Attack
- Skill 1 (tongue pull)
- Skill 2 (jump attack)

You already have all three moves. The game just points at them one by one.

### Step 5 — Second Fight

After the Mosquito dies...

A **swarm** of mosquitoes shows up.

Same moves. More pressure.

### Step 6 — First Real Objective

You clear the swarm.

The game shows you a bigger problem.

You get your first objective:

> "Investigate the disturbance"

A marker points to a general area. Not an exact path.

### Step 7 — Rats

You follow the marker.

You find:
- Burrows
- Tracks
- Scared animals
- Then: **rats**

You fight them.

*(Note: right now this is one straight path. No real choice here yet. This is flagged as something to improve later.)*

### Step 8 — Save the Native Frog

You find another frog. A real one. In danger.

You:
1. Clear the rats near it
2. Fix its habitat
3. Watch it move to safety

This whole part is short. About 2-4 minutes.

While fixing the habitat, Sterling detects something.

A **biological signal**.

### Step 9 — First Transformation

You follow the signal.

You find the **Salamander Genome**.

You interact with it.

You transform. No loading. No delay.

### Step 10 — Learn Salamander (No Tutorial)

You get free time to explore.

No forced tutorial here.

Sterling reacts naturally, but never explains your buttons.

You learn by playing.

Salamander's kit:
- **Basic Attack** — Bite
- **Skill 1** — Regenerative Skin (heals you over time)
- **Skill 2** — Toxic Secretion (poison zone)

Poison can stack up to 3 times. No more than that.

### Step 11 — The Snake

You meet a **Snake**.

It doesn't fight fair.

It hides. Strikes. Runs. Repeats.

This is the challenge — not high HP, just smart behavior.

### Step 12 — Contaminated Water

You find a badly damaged water source.

Weak or dead animals nearby.

Something big did this.

You investigate.

Sterling detects another signal.

### Step 13 — Second Transformation

You find the **Water Monitor Genome**.

You transform again.

Water Monitor's kit:
- **Basic Attack** — Claw Slash
- **Skill 1** — Tail Sweep (wide hit, knockback)
- **Skill 2** — Crushing Bite (big damage, good vs bosses)

This form feels heavy. Strong. Tanky.

### Step 14 — First Big Obstacle

A fallen tree blocks your path.

You push it. Pure strength moment.

More enemies show up. You test your new kit.

You find large tracks. Something bigger lives here.

### Step 15 — The Nutria Colony

A damaged riverbank. Full of burrows.

Fight order:
1. Normal Nutria
2. A group of them
3. A **Colony Defender** (mini-boss, not the final boss)

### Step 16 — THE CHIMERA (Final Boss of Wetlands)

The area goes quiet.

Something huge appears:

**The Snail–Cane Toad Chimera.**

This is what your leaked genome energy created.

Three phases:

**Phase 1**
- Body Rush
- Tongue Strike
- Shell Roll
- Occasional Toxic Burst
- This phase teaches you the fight.

**Phase 2 — "Toxic Territory"**
- Toxic Splash
- Toxic Tongue
- Shell Slam
- Creates a toxic zone on the ground
- You must clear this zone eventually

**Phase 3 — Hardest**
- Crushing Charge
- Shell Crush
- Toad Lunge
- A dangerous combo: charge → turn → slam → opening

You win by hitting **weak points**:
- Throat
- Underside
- Body opening

These are fixed, learnable moments. Not random luck.

### Step 17 — Genome Core

The Chimera falls.

You approach.

Suit scans automatically.

**GENOME CORE DETECTED.**

You interact. You extract it.

No minigame.

### Step 18 — Restore the Wetlands

You clean up:
- Clear debris
- Run the Purification Module

You watch it happen:
- Water clears
- Plants return
- Animals come back

This is real. Not a menu screen.

## THE TRANSITION

You walk from Wetlands into Rainforest.

No loading screen. Just walking.

Marsh → river → floodplain → forest edge → deep forest.

At the border, you choose:

**One Legacy Genome.**

Pick ONE of your three Wetlands forms.

Only that one comes with you.

This choice matters.
