# GENOMORPH — FULL GAME FLOW

## 0. PROLOGUE — WHY THE PLAYER BECOMES A FROG

**Status: LOCKED / already made**

The prologue establishes:

* the ecological collapse
* Dr. Sterling
* the GenoMorph project
* why the Volunteer was chosen
* the suit's genetic compatibility
* the first transformation
* the DSEE overload
* the 12 genomes being released
* the Purification Module surviving
* the player becoming a Frog
* the mystery of where the genomes went

The prologue ends:

> **STERLING (RADIO):** "Your suit is locked to Frog form for now. Go carefully, Volunteer. Whatever is out there — it isn't ordinary anymore."

Then:

# GAMEPLAY BEGINS

---

# ACT I — WETLANDS

## Q01 — FIRST AWAKENING

**Purpose:** Teach the player how to exist as an animal.

The player wakes in the mud as the Small Native Frog.

### Gameplay

**Movement**

The player learns:

* movement
* automatic hopping
* camera
* stopping/idle animation

Sterling checks the radio.

The player explores the immediate area.

They see:

* polluted water
* damaged vegetation
* dead areas
* invasive organisms

The player investigates something.

This introduces:

**Interact → Information → Optional Learn More → Database**

Then the first mosquito appears.

### First combat

Mosquito:

* basic attack

More mosquitoes:

* Sticky Tongue Whip
* Splashdown Jump

The player isn't told everything through a giant tutorial screen.

They **discover the abilities through use**.

### Quest progression

The player investigates a larger disturbance.

They encounter the first invasive Rats.

---

# Q02 — INVASIVE RAT

The player enters increasingly damaged wetlands.

They see the rats **before fighting them**.

The player investigates:

* burrows
* damaged vegetation
* contaminated areas

Then native wildlife appears.

The rats disturb the native animal.

The player intervenes.

### Combat

Small rat group.

Player learns:

> Invasive species aren't just enemies. They are affecting the ecosystem.

After defeating them, the rats flee.

The disturbance continues deeper into the wetlands.

---

# Q03 — FRAGILE HABITAT

The player finds a remaining patch of relatively healthy habitat.

Native frogs are there.

Rats are threatening them.

### Objective

> **CLEAR THE INVASIVE RATS**

Player fights.

Afterward:

> **RESTORE THE DAMAGED HABITAT**

The player interacts with the critical damaged area.

The Purification Module activates.

The area begins recovering.

Water clears.

Vegetation begins returning.

Wildlife starts coming back.

Then:

**BIOLOGICAL SIGNAL DETECTED**

The player follows it.

### First transformation

The player discovers:

# SALAMANDER GENOME

Interact.

Short genome presentation.

> **TRANSFORM**

The player becomes Salamander.

---

# Q04 — THE SALAMANDER

This is not another long tutorial.

The player immediately gets control.

They move.

They fight.

They take damage.

Then they notice:

**Health is recovering.**

Sterling comments on the regeneration.

The player continues into more contaminated terrain.

### Salamander gameplay

The player discovers:

* Bite
* Regenerative Skin
* Toxic Secretion
* poison stacking
* survivability

The player doesn't need a long explanation.

They **experience the difference from Frog**.

The objective remains:

> **FOLLOW THE BIOLOGICAL SIGNAL**

The environment becomes increasingly contaminated.

---

# Q05 — THE CONTAMINATED WATER

The player encounters an invasive Snake.

The Snake:

* hides
* attacks
* retreats
* attacks again

The player follows it.

This leads to disturbed native wildlife.

The player investigates.

Eventually they reach heavily contaminated water.

The environment is visibly unhealthy.

The suit detects another strong biological signal.

The player follows it.

---

# WATER MONITOR DISCOVERY

The player finds:

# WATER MONITOR GENOME

Transform.

Now the player becomes much larger and heavier.

Immediately:

### Physical test

A fallen tree blocks the route.

The Water Monitor pushes/clears it.

Then invasive creatures attack.

### Combat

Player learns:

* Claw Slash
* Tail Sweep
* Crushing Bite

A larger enemy gives Crushing Bite a clear purpose.

After combat:

The player discovers extensive large-animal disturbance.

---

# Q06 — NUTRIA COLONY

The player reaches a damaged wetland bank.

They observe:

* nutria
* burrows
* eroded bank
* damaged vegetation

Investigation reveals the ecological effect.

Then combat begins.

Normal nutria first.

After enough are defeated:

## COLONY DEFENDER

A stronger nutria emerges.

Not a Chimera.

Just a powerful colony defender.

Player defeats it.

The remaining nutria flee.

Then...

**Silence.**

Wildlife disappears.

The ground shakes.

**THUD.**

**THUD.**

Something huge approaches.

Sterling:

> "Volunteer..."

Another signal.

> "That's not one of the genomes."

Then:

# WETLANDS CHIMERA

---

# ACT II — WETLANDS CHIMERA

## SNAIL–CANE TOAD CHIMERA

This is the major payoff of Act I.

The player enters a natural wetland arena.

No artificial walls.

The player can switch between:

* Frog
* Salamander
* Water Monitor

with the transformation cooldown.

---

## PHASE 1 — CHASE / MOBILITY

Approx. 2 minutes.

Chimera uses:

* Body Rush
* Tongue Strike
* Shell Roll
* occasional Toxic Slime Burst

The player learns:

> Attack → dodge/position → vulnerability → attack.

Frog is advantageous.

But not required.

---

## PHASE 2 — TOXIC TERRITORY

Approx. 2 minutes.

The Chimera changes behavior.

It starts controlling the battlefield.

Temporary toxic zones appear.

Major attacks create larger zones.

The player has to manage positioning.

Salamander becomes attractive.

But every species remains usable.

---

## PHASE 3 — BRUTAL CLASH

Approx. 2 minutes.

The Chimera becomes aggressive.

Attacks:

* Crushing Charge
* Shell Crush
* Toad Lunge
* Frenzied Rampage

Water Monitor becomes advantageous.

Again, not mandatory.

The Chimera becomes increasingly unstable.

Player exploits vulnerability windows.

---

# CHIMERA DEFEATED

The Chimera collapses.

The player approaches.

Automatic scan.

> **GENOME CORE DETECTED**

Player presses Interact.

Purification Module extracts it.

# GENOME CORE RECOVERED

Sterling realizes the signal points toward another ecosystem.

---

# WETLAND RESTORATION

Player reaches the critical damaged habitat.

Short restoration interaction.

The ecosystem begins recovering.

Not instantly becoming perfect.

Instead:

* water begins flowing
* vegetation begins recovering
* insects return
* wildlife returns

Sterling:

> "We didn't rebuild this."

> "We just gave it a chance to rebuild itself."

---

# ACT III — WETLAND → RAINFOREST

This is a **playable transition**, not a loading screen.

The player travels:

**wetland → riverbank → floodplain → forest edge → rainforest**

Wildlife begins moving between ecosystems.

The player follows the Genome Core signal.

Eventually:

# ECOSYSTEM TRANSITION DETECTED

The player must select one:

* Frog
* Salamander
* Water Monitor

as the:

# LEGACY GENOME

The other genomes remain stored.

The player crosses into the Rainforest.

---

# ACT IV — RAINFOREST

## Q07 — ABANDONED LOGGING SITE

The player enters the rainforest.

At first, it looks healthy.

Then:

**one stump.**

Then another.

Then:

* sawdust
* broken branches
* damaged vegetation
* piles of logs
* disturbed soil

The player reaches the abandoned logging site.

No humans need to appear.

The environment tells the story.

---

# Q08 — DISPLACED WILDLIFE

The player follows animal tracks.

They discover a damaged feeding ground.

Animals are searching for food.

The player investigates.

Then they find one native animal trapped by fallen timber.

The player clears the obstruction.

The animal escapes.

The player realizes the consequences:

**Forest destroyed → food lost → habitat disrupted → animals displaced.**

The Genome Core detects another signal.

---

# Q09 — WILD BOAR GENOME

The player follows the signal.

They encounter disturbed soil and uprooted plants.

A Wild Boar crosses the player's path.

The suit reacts.

The player discovers:

# WILD BOAR GENOME

Transform.

Immediately:

### Physical obstacle

Large fallen tree blocks the route.

The Wild Boar breaks through.

### Combat

Invasive creatures attack.

The player learns:

* Headbutt
* Tusk Strike
* Front Hoof Slam

The core identity becomes:

> **Momentum + power + charging**

---

# RAINFOREST PROGRESSION

From here the game repeats the successful structure established in Wetlands, but with **different ecological problems and different animal gameplay**.

The player doesn't simply repeat:

> "Find animal → fight animal → find animal."

Instead:

### Wild Boar section

Focus:

**Deforestation + habitat fragmentation**

Player deals with:

* blocked paths
* invasive animals
* damaged forest
* physical environmental obstacles

Eventually the next biological signal appears.

---

# PANGOLIN

The player discovers the Pangolin Genome.

Transformation.

The gameplay changes completely.

Pangolin's identity becomes:

**armor + defense + specialized movement**

The player encounters situations where surviving/defending is more important than charging.

The rainforest's problem also evolves.

The player discovers consequences deeper inside the forest.

---

# EAGLE

The player eventually reaches the Eagle Genome.

Transformation.

Now the player gets access to a completely different perspective of the rainforest.

The Eagle isn't simply:

> "Wild Boar but stronger."

Instead:

**Aerial exploration + observation + aerial combat**

The player can see environmental damage from above.

This can reveal:

* fragmented forest
* destroyed canopy
* isolated habitats
* larger Chimera activity

---

# RAINFOREST CHIMERA

After the player has experienced:

**Wild Boar → Pangolin → Eagle**

the Rainforest Chimera becomes the major payoff.

Its design should react to the player's current animal just like the Wetlands Chimera.

The battle has three phases.

Each phase emphasizes a different ecological/biological challenge.

The player can switch between the three Rainforest species.

Defeat the Chimera.

Recover:

# RAINFOREST GENOME CORE

Then restore the damaged rainforest habitat.

---

# ACT V — RAINFOREST → MOUNTAIN

Same philosophy.

No teleport.

The player physically travels out of the rainforest.

The environment gradually changes:

**Rainforest → foothills → rocky terrain → mountain**

Wildlife behavior helps communicate the transition.

At the ecological boundary:

# SELECT LEGACY GENOME

Choose:

* Wild Boar
* Pangolin
* Eagle

One becomes the Mountain Legacy Genome.

---

# ACT VI — MOUNTAIN

The Mountain ecosystem introduces a different environmental problem.

The three species are:

## TAMARAW

Identity:

**Power + territorial combat + rugged terrain**

The player learns the animal through direct gameplay.

---

## BEAR

Identity:

**Heavy power + versatility**

The player encounters larger threats and environmental challenges.

Bear shouldn't simply replace Tamaraw.

It offers different tactical options.

---

## GOAT

Identity:

**Mountain mobility + difficult terrain**

The mountain itself becomes a major gameplay element.

The player learns:

> Animals survive because their bodies are adapted to their environment.

This is important because it reinforces the central GenoMorph concept.

---

# MOUNTAIN CHIMERA

After all three Mountain genomes have been introduced:

**Mountain Chimera**

Three phases again.

The player can freely switch between:

* Tamaraw
* Bear
* Goat
* Legacy species

depending on the established rules.

The Chimera represents the ecological crisis reaching its final major escalation.

Player defeats it.

Recover:

# FINAL GENOME CORE

---

# ACT VII — FINAL RESTORATION

Now the player has travelled through:

**Wetlands → Rainforest → Mountain**

and recovered the major Genome Cores.

But the world isn't automatically fixed.

The player must return to the central problem.

The final section reveals that the Chimera incidents were not isolated events.

They were consequences of the original genome discharge and the already-damaged ecosystems.

The player now has enough recovered biological data to stabilize the GenoMorph system.

---

# FINAL STORY REVELATION

Sterling has been studying everything remotely.

The player discovers that the suit wasn't simply malfunctioning.

The DSEE accident created an uncontrolled biological chain reaction.

The damaged ecosystems made the released genomes much more dangerous.

Sterling realizes something important:

**The solution isn't to make the player permanently transform into one "ultimate" animal.**

The solution is understanding:

> **adaptation depends on environment.**

That's the philosophical conclusion of the game.

---

# FINAL GAMEPLAY

The player uses the knowledge and abilities acquired throughout the game.

Instead of one final super-form, the player relies on **different species for different ecological situations**.

The final restoration involves using the ecosystem itself rather than simply defeating another enemy.

The player helps stabilize:

* Wetlands
* Rainforest
* Mountain

The environments visibly recover.

---

# ENDING

The player returns to the lab area.

Sterling finally sees the Volunteer again.

This mirrors the beginning.

But now:

At the beginning, Sterling said:

> "The ecosystem already knows how to fix itself."

At the end, the player has actually proven it.

The GenoMorph project wasn't about controlling nature.

It became a way of **understanding and adapting to it**.

## The important pacing structure

The game should **not** feel like three enormous RPG chapters.

Each ecosystem follows a relatively compact rhythm:

**Discover problem**
→ **Investigate**
→ **Experience ecological consequence**
→ **Fight**
→ **Discover new genome**
→ **Transform**
→ **Learn the new animal through play**
→ **Escalate environmental problem**
→ **Third animal**
→ **Chimera**
→ **Restore ecosystem**
→ **Travel to next ecosystem**

That's the backbone.

And importantly, the **transformation is the reward/payoff**, while the **Chimera is the biome climax**.
